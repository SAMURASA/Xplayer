This player can slow down and reverb songs in live mode, and it also has a radio feature, song logo display, and when you click on a track name, the lyrics will open (if the song is found). You can adjust the slowdown and reverb settings in the settings. Additionally, the player will display what you're listening to in Discord.
<br>
<b>To use the player, go to the settings and select a folder with songs in the first field. The second field is for displaying album logos of songs in Discord. Once you've selected a folder, you can close the settings and start listening to your songs.</b>
